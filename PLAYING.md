# Rage: Clay Demo

1. Extract the entire ZIP into one folder on a Windows 10/11 64-bit PC.
2. Double-click **Rage Demo.exe**.
3. Choose Play. Your first expedition includes the village story.

No Unity Editor, Python, developer tools, installation, or separate original-game download is required. Keep the executable, GameJam3_Rage_Data, and MonoBleedingEdge folders together.

## Controls

Move: A/D or arrows. Jump: Space (hold for height). Kick: tap Enter or left mouse. Shoot: hold and release attack. Talk/continue: E. Pause: Esc. Dash: Shift after buying Clay dash.

Controller: left stick move, A jump, X attack, Y talk, B unlocked dash, Start pause. B also goes back in menus.

## Your expedition

Explore six districts: Paper Lane, Sunflower Steps, Glaze Alley, Mossy Rooftops, The Clayworks, and The Royal Kiln. Defeat all enemies in a district to open its exit. Cleared districts remain unlocked in Choose district.

Damage and enemy defeats earn saved clay. Spend it in the Workshop on four body upgrades and four tricks. Purchases apply on the next expedition. Defeat keeps clay, upgrades, and unlocked districts.

Purple lobbers throw arcing shots. Blue shieldguards resist attacks from the front. Green menders heal nearby foes. Pink splitters turn into two fast rushlings. The Kiln King telegraphs slams, volleys, and charges, then summons help in phase two. Jump over low shots and use your dash to escape danger.

This demo is offline and single-player. Saves and settings use Unity PlayerPrefs for DefaultCompany/GameJam3_Rage. Closing and relaunching preserves progression.
